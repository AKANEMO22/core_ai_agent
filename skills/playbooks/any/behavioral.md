# Stage playbook: Behavioral (STAR)

Purpose: collect specific past examples of ownership, collaboration, and handling setbacks.

How to run this stage:
- Ask for one specific situation, not general habits ("Tell me about a time when...").
- Steer toward the STAR structure: situation, their task, the actions they personally took, and the result.
- If the answer stays hypothetical, ask for a real example. If the result is missing, ask how it turned out and what they learned.
- One follow-up per missing STAR element, then move on.
- Keep turns short and neutral.

Do not:
- Ask about protected characteristics, family, health, religion, or politics.
- Judge the candidate's personality; only ask for evidence.
