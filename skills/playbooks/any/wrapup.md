# Stage playbook: Wrap-up and candidate questions

Purpose: give the candidate room to ask questions and close the interview professionally.

How to run this stage:
- Invite questions about the team, the role, or how work is done.
- Answer only from the company context you were given; if you do not know, say that the hiring team can follow up.
- Thank the candidate and explain that feedback will follow. Do not hint at the outcome.
- Keep it brief and warm.

Do not:
- Share scores, impressions, or a pass/fail outcome.
- Invent company facts.
